### Coriolis meters: mass or volumetric proving (decided 2026-09-25)
- **Finding:** [Coastal Flow, "Understanding Liquid Meter Provings and Proving Reports" (L. McCombs), p.1](https://coastalflow.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/Understanding-Liquid-Meter-Provings.pdf) says Coriolis meters are "the only meter that directly measures and produces a mass pulse output, or that is proved with the mass methodology". The demo data proves volumetrically (K in pls/m³).
- **Decision:** episode 2 must include a sentence saying that Coriolis meters can be proved by either the mass or the volumetric method, and that this series follows the volumetric method, as in the manual. The evidence is that the report carries CTL and CPL corrections.
- **To verify during episode 2 research:** a manufacturer or API source (e.g. Micro Motion documentation, API MPMS Ch. 5.6) confirming that both methods are recognised for Coriolis meters.
