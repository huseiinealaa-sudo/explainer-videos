# scene_gallery — source

The content of this video is the scene library itself: `explainer/scenes.py` (module
docstring and `LIBRARY`). Each narration segment names one function and says what it
draws; nothing is taken from outside the repository, so no research notes are needed.

| # | Function | What the clip shows |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | title_card | series line, title, rule, subtitle |
| 2 | section_title | corner heading, then transformed into the next heading |
| 3 | bullet_list | heading, then three points on their word cues |
| 4 | equation | Text-piece equation, one part indicated, one part framed |
| 5 | worked_calculation | formula → substituted values → boxed result |
| 6 | labeled_diagram | tank, pump, gauge drawing with numbered callouts |
| 7 | process_flow | four boxes with arrows, current step lit |
| 8 | stage_bar | five stages, marker moving across |
| 9 | data_table | three rows, out-of-tolerance row marked |
| 10 | comparison | two cards, verdict tick |
| 11 | line_chart | six readings, acceptance band, outlier in red |
| 12 | bar_chart | four bars, limit line, bar over the limit in red |
| 13 | checklist | two ticks, one cross |
| 14 | summary_box | framed takeaways |
| 15 | concept_map | central idea with five linked ideas |
| 16 | timeline | four steps along an arrow |
| 17 | image_panel | own SVG sketch with caption and credit |
| 18 | document_panel | monospaced report sheet, out-of-tolerance line framed with highlight() |
