# symbol_gallery — source

The content of this video is the library itself: `explainer/icons.py` (Tabler icons,
vendored in `explainer/assets/tabler/`, MIT, version in `TABLER_VERSION`) and
`explainer/symbols/isa.py` (ISA-5.1 symbols drawn in code). The references behind every
ISA drawing are listed per symbol in `explainer/symbols/SOURCES.md`; the video adds no
other claim. No real plant, device or loop number is shown: FT-101, FIC-101, FY-101,
FV-101, PIC-102, PY-103 and LSH-104 are illustrative tags.
