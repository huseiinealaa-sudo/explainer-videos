# The one approval message (Arabic, to the owner)

One message, before any rendering, with these parts in this order:

1. **Plan**: episodes (or segments of a single video) with titles and target durations,
   sized from the material inventory (2–4 min per main section of the source).
2. **Storyboard**: the table of each episode (`storyboard_template.md`), or a link to the
   committed storyboard files if they are long.
3. **Narration**: the full diacritized text of every segment, numbered as in the script.
   Mark every sentence added or corrected from research with [+] and its source.
4. **Quality gate** (per episode):

   | Episode | Worked examples | Custom drawings | Text-scene share | Left out (reason → proposal) |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | 2 | 4 | 25 % | — |
   | 2 | 1 | 3 | 38 % ⚠ | section 4.3 not verifiable → keep as "per the manual", or drop? |

   A share above about one third is marked and explained, with a proposal to bring it down.
5. **Source conflicts and new values**: each with the options and a recommendation.
6. **Questions** that block production, if any (otherwise none).

After the approval, no further confirmation is asked except the first-episode review of a
series (skill step d.4).
